Source licenses checked 19 November 2026. Wikimedia describes both additional
recordings as the respective uploaders' own work dedicated under CC0. Source
Ogg SHA-1: `a1fa4fd942401922c5c3301816384aae86956522` (Athan),
`563ec4bbc12c8a67ddb5c96adce597fdb526f988` (Iqama).

Android raw-resource copies are byte-identical to their `asset/` counterparts.
Full recordings are available for preview and Android alarm playback. iOS local
notification sounds must be under 30 seconds, so the new recordings use the
explicitly labelled excerpts; Classic Athan uses the gentle chime on iOS.
Imported PCM WAV files under 30 seconds can be used directly by iOS notifications;
other imports use a short beep there and remain available for full previews.
